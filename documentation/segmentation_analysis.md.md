# RFM Analysis — Phase 3 Segmentation Results

## Segment Distribution

| Segment | Customers | % of Total | Avg Recency | Avg Frequency | Avg Spend | Total Revenue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loyal Customers | 15,191 | 16.27% | 388 days | 1 | 313.65 | 4,764,635 |
| Potential Loyalists | 24,814 | 26.58% | 67 days | 1 | 163.88 | 4,066,528 |
| At Risk | 23,203 | 24.85% | 394 days | 1 | 80.41 | 1,865,816 |
| Others | 14,841 | 15.90% | 222 days | 1 | 94.65 | 1,404,762 |
| New Customers | 12,075 | 12.93% | 140 days | 1 | 164.46 | 1,985,801 |
| Big Spenders | 3,124 | 3.35% | 221 days | 1 | 410.38 | 1,282,034 |
| Champions | 109 | 0.12% | 51 days | 2 | 485.16 | 52,882 |

## Key Findings

1. *Champions are extremely rare — only 109 customers
(0.12%) qualify. These are the most recently active,
most frequent, and highest spending customers.
Losing even a handful would hurt revenue significantly.*
2. *At Risk is the most dangerous segment — 23,203
customers (24.85%) used to buy but haven't recently.
Nearly 1 in 4 customers is slipping away right now.*
3. *Potential Loyalists are the biggest opportunity —
24,814 customers bought recently but only once.
Converting even 10% to loyal customers would
significantly grow revenue.*
4. *Big Spenders are hidden gems — only 3.35% of
customers but average spend of 410 per order.
They spend like Champions but don't buy as frequently.*

## Marketing Recommendations by Segment

| Segment | Priority | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champions | 🔴 Critical | VIP treatment — exclusive offers, early access, loyalty rewards |
| Big Spenders | 🔴 High | Personal outreach — up-sell premium products |
| At Risk | 🟠 High | Win-back campaign — discount or personalised offer immediately |
| Potential Loyalists | 🟡 Medium | Second purchase incentive — targeted email within 30 days |
| New Customers | 🟡 Medium | On boarding sequence — guide them to second purchase |
| Loyal Customers | 🟢 Maintain | Regular engagement — loyalty program, referral rewards |
| Others | ⚪ Low | Generic newsletter — low budget allocation |

## The Story the Data Tells

*The business has a concentration problem.*

*Its most valuable customers — Champions and Big
Spenders — represent only 3.47% of the customer
base but generate the highest value per transaction.*

*Meanwhile 24.85% of customers are At Risk.
These are people who spent money on the platform,
had a decent experience, but were never brought back.*

*The marketing budget question answers itself:
Stop spending on acquiring new one-time buyers.
Start spending on keeping the 3.47% who matter most
and re-engaging the 24.85% who are slipping away.*
